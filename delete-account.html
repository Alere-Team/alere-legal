Delete your Aleré account and data

This page is for people who use the Aleré app by Aleré Labs. You can delete your account and every piece of data Aleré holds about you at any time, from inside the app, with no email or support ticket needed.

Steps to delete your account in the app

  1. Open Aleré and tap your initial in the top-right corner to open Profile.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account & Data.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, and email and password if you added them).
  • Your health profile: age, sex, measurements, lab values, lifestyle answers, dietary identity, goals and medications.
  • Your Metabolic Twin timeline: every meal scan, glucose estimate, check-in and lab entry.
  • Your chat history with Alis and the patterns Alis learned about you.
  • Your community profile, friend links, streaks and points.
  • The private server backup of all of the above, if backup was on.

Everything above is removed from the phone and from Aleré's server at the moment you confirm. Nothing is kept.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

  • Any single meal, check-in or lab entry can be deleted from your Metabolic Twin timeline (tap the entry, then Delete).
  • Profile → Back up & sync → off deletes the server copy of your data while keeping it on your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone; if backup was on, turn it off first to remove the server copy too.

If you can no longer open the app

Email support@alerelabs.ai from the address you used in the app (or tell us the name you signed up with) and ask for deletion. We delete the server copy and confirm by reply. There is no retention period.
