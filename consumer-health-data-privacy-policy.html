Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-25
Version: 1.0

This is Aleré's standalone Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy, required by Washington's My Health My Data Act (MHMDA) and similar state consumer-health-data laws. It is separate from — and more detailed than, for health data specifically — Aleré's general Privacy Policy. Aleré is a cash-pay wellness app; because it does not bill insurance, it is not a HIPAA covered entity, so this policy — not HIPAA — is what governs how your health data is handled.

What counts as "consumer health data" here

Under MHMDA, consumer health data is personal information that identifies a consumer's physical or mental health status. In Aleré, that includes the categories in the table below.

Categories, sources, and purposes

CategorySourcePurpose
Health profile — age, sex, height, weight, waist/hip measurements, lab values (e.g. fasting glucose, HbA1c), lifestyle answers, dietary identity, goals Entered by you during onboarding Estimate insulin sensitivity and personalize wellness guidance
Health history — steps, sleep, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, glucose readings, body measurements Apple Health / Health Connect, if you connect them Personalize wellness estimates and companion insights
Meal log and photos — meal entries, macros, predicted glucose response, meal photos Logged or photographed by you Estimate glucose response to meals; build your meal history
Scans and bio-logs — CGM screenshots, lab-report photos, typed or spoken health notes Captured by you when you use a scan/log feature Extract structured health values for your record and analysis (only when AI analysis is enabled and you consent)
Chat content with health context — messages you send the Alis assistant Typed by you Answer your questions and give in-context guidance
Calendar-derived stress category — a redacted event category (e.g. "meeting") and time, never the title/notes/location/attendees Your device calendar, only if you opt in Notice recurring-event / stress patterns, on-device only
Community progress numbers — streak, points, metabolic-age trend Derived from your app usage, only if you join Community Show your progress to friends who accepted your request, while you keep sharing on

Who this data is shared with

  • Google (Gemini API) — the specific content you submit for a scan or chat (meal photos, CGM/lab images, bio-log text or audio, chat messages) is sent to Google's Gemini API for one-time processing, either directly or by way of Aleré's own backend relay, only after you consent, and only while AI features are enabled. Google processes it under Google's own API terms and privacy policy.
  • Supabase — Aleré's database and backend host (United States), acting strictly as Aleré's data processor. Supabase stores the encrypted backup of your health profile, health history, meal log, and learned patterns, and (if you join Community) your Community profile and shared stats.
  • Nobody else. Aleré does not share, disclose, or sell consumer health data to advertisers, data brokers, analytics vendors, or any other third party. There is no "sale" pathway in the app.

Consent

Collecting your health data and sharing specific content with Google for AI analysis are treated as separate consents, never bundled into one blanket agreement:

  • You are asked to agree before the first AI request that would send your content to Google's Gemini API; you can decline and keep using the app's non-AI features.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time by turning AI features off; no further content is sent to Google after withdrawal.
  • Aleré does not sell consumer health data, so there is no separate "sale" consent to give.

Your rights and how to exercise them, in the app

  1. Access / know what we hold — Profile → Export / Download your data produces a file with your health profile, health record, meal log, health history, and learned patterns. (Chat messages and photo files are not included in the export file.)
  2. Delete — Profile → Delete Account & Data deletes the server backup of your health data, then permanently erases your account, health profile, meal timeline, and chat history from your device. If you joined Community, contact us (below) to remove that separate Community account.
  3. Withdraw consent — turn AI features off at any time (the setting that governs whether content is sent to Google); turn off Back up & sync to stop and delete the server backup; leave Community or turn off sharing to stop sharing progress numbers.
  4. Appeal — if you believe a request was not honored, contact us at the address below.

Deletion timing: in the app, Delete Account & Data takes effect immediately. As a backstop commitment consistent with MHMDA, any consumer health data deletion request will be completed within 45 days at the outside.

Health disclaimer

Aleré provides wellness estimates for informational purposes only. Predicted glucose responses and other outputs are estimates, not measurements, and Aleré is not a medical device. Aleré does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Consult a healthcare professional before making health decisions.

Retention

Your health data is retained on your device, and in the server backup described above, for as long as you use the app and keep backup enabled. It is deleted on request as described above, and it is not retained longer than needed for the purposes listed in this policy.

Children

Aleré is not directed at children under 13 and should not be used by them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the "Effective date" above and, where required, notify you in the app.
